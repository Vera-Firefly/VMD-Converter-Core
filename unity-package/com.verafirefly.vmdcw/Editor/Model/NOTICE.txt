MMD Standard Humanoid Reference Rig
Copyright 2026 MMD Play Mode Preview contributors

This product includes a mesh-free PMX reference rig whose standard-bone and IK
positions are calibrated to a local Miku compatibility model. It contains one
tiny transparent compatibility triangle and material required by the legacy
PMX2FBX backend, but no character mesh, texture, material, physics, or
character-specific bones from that model.
